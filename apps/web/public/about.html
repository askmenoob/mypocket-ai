How it works

A simpler way to organise everyday financial records

MyPocket AI combines transaction automation, messaging tools and Google services to help users maintain clear, accessible financial records.
1

Sign in securely

Users sign in with their Google Account to authenticate their identity and access their MyPocket AI workspace.

2

Connect Google services

Users authorise MyPocket AI to create or manage the Google Sheets and Google Drive files required for their financial records.

3

Record and review

Expenses and transactions are organised automatically, saved to the user's files and made available through the MyPocket AI dashboard.

Transparent Google data use

Why MyPocket AI requests Google access

Google access is requested only to provide features chosen by the user. The permissions requested are displayed during Google's authorisation process before access is granted.

  • Google Account information: used for secure authentication and identification of the user's MyPocket AI account.
  • Google Sheets: used to create and update transaction records, categories, summaries and financial information requested by the user.
  • Google Drive: used to create and manage files required by MyPocket AI, including authorised spreadsheet and receipt-related files.
  • User control: access can be disconnected from MyPocket AI or revoked through the user's Google Account settings.
  • No sale of Google user data: MyPocket AI does not sell Google user data or use it for personalised advertising.

Privacy-first by design

MyPocket AI is designed around user control. Financial records are maintained in Google files connected or created with the user's authorisation. Our Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored and protected.
