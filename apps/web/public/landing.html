FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I record transactions?

Send a WhatsApp message like “nasi lemak rm5 tng” and MyPocket AI will record it into your Google Sheet.

Do you store my financial data?

MyPocket AI is designed to use Google Sheets in your own Google Drive as the source of truth.

Where is my data stored?

Your finance spreadsheet is created in your Google Drive. You can view and manage it directly.

Can I use MyPocket AI for business?

Yes. Business package supports finance workflows for teams and business tracking.

Do you support Bahasa Malaysia?

Yes. MyPocket AI can understand common Malay and English transaction formats.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Packages are designed to be flexible and can be changed as your needs change.
