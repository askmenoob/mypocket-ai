MyPocket AI

MyPocket AI is a finance automation platform that helps individuals, families, and businesses record expenses and income through WhatsApp, organise transactions in Google Sheets, and review finance activity from a secure web dashboard.

What MyPocket AI does

  • Records expense and income transactions from WhatsApp messages.
  • Creates user-owned Google Sheets in the user's Google Drive.
  • Syncs transaction records to Google Sheets for easy review and export.
  • Provides a dashboard for workspace setup, WhatsApp bot pairing, and finance summaries.
  • Supports personal, family, and business finance workflows.

How MyPocket AI uses Google data

MyPocket AI requests Google Drive and Google Sheets access only to create, read, update, and sync MyPocket finance spreadsheets owned by the user. MyPocket AI does not sell Google user data.
