MyPocket AI

MyPocket AI is a finance automation platform that helps individuals, families, and businesses record expenses through WhatsApp, organise transactions in Google Sheets, and manage finance activity from a web dashboard.

What MyPocket AI does

  • Records expense and income transactions from WhatsApp messages.
  • Creates and syncs user-owned Google Sheets in the user's Google Drive.
  • Provides a dashboard for reviewing transactions, workspace setup, and integrations.
  • Supports personal, family, and business finance workflows.

Google data usage

MyPocket AI requests Google Drive and Google Sheets access only to create, read, update, and sync the user's own MyPocket finance spreadsheets. MyPocket AI does not sell Google user data.
